pDoom Shadow Score

Every daily run now scores the headlines twice. The published number still comes from the single-number scorer (five samples, trimmed mean, regime filter). The shadow scores nine fronts separately on a 0–100 scale and computes a composite in code. Only the published number reaches the app; the shadow is recorded in doom.shadow so the rubric's level and its breadth weight can be set from real input before it goes live. Decision point: about 30 production days, mid-November.

The worst front sets the floor; breadth lifts it: max + 0.25 × mean(other fronts above 10). One front at 80 with the rest quiet reads 80; everything at 55 reads about 66.

Power mean with exponent 4, equal weights. Leans on the worst fronts but compresses when most are quiet: one 80 with the rest at 10 reads about 46.
